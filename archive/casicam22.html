Dear Colleagues,We have the great pleasure to announce the organization of the second edition of CASABLANCA INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING,which will be held in Morocco on November 23 and 24, 2022.

CASICAM’22 is an opportunity for researchers, scientists, representatives of R&D organizations, engineers, industrialists, entrepreneurs, managers, doctors and architects to interact and share their research activities, ideas, developments as well as the emerging applications of Additive Manufacturing and related technologies. The conference will feature renowned keynote speakers in several key areas.Moreover, the conference offers a valuable platform to create new contacts in the field of 3D Printing & Additive Manufacturing, by providing valuable networking time for you to meet great personnel in the field.

We look forward to seeing you at CASICAM’22 in Casablanca, Morocco.

Conference language

The official language of CASICAM’22 is English and simultaneous interpretation is not provided. It is therefore expected that authors can present their work fluently in the English language.

CONFERENCE TOPICS

  • Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFAM).
  • Functionally Graded Additive Manufacturing (FGAM).
  • New and innovative materials for AM.
  • AM Parts/processes modeling and simulation.
  • AM Process optimisation, monitoring and qualification.
  • 4D printing.
  • AM post-processing operations.
  • AM product metrology and quality control.
  • AM standards and certification.
  • Health, Safety and Environment challenges.
  • Education, training and research strategy.
  • AM Applications and challenges.

SUBMISSION

Guidelines for paper submission

Prospective authors are invited to submit full and original research papers wich are not submitted or published or under consideration anywhere in other conference or journal.

All submissions should be written in English with a maximum length of 08 printed pages standard LNCS format including figures without incurring additional page charges (maximum 2 additional pages, with over-length page fee).

All submitted papers must conform to the CASICAM’22-Paper. Authors are invited to use either the Latex template or MS Word Template available via this Link:CASICAM’22-Paper Template CASICAM’22 template for MS Word

The full papers should be submitted in PDF format, via the CASICAM’22 EasyChair online.

You are advised to use the online submission system and create an account by following the instructions. Authors will be informed of the acceptance of their papers within the time frame set above.

Submit your paper

In case you need an invitation letter, please contact us at contact@casicam.com

Guidelines for preparation and sending of presentations

  • All registered authors with accepted papers should prepare and send their presentation. This is a prerequisite for a paper to be included in the Conference Programme.
  • Please note that the duration of the presentation should be strictly 15 minutes.
  • Please follow the template below for your PowerPoint presentation: CASICAM’22 template for your PowerPoint presentation
  • Use simple (Arial, Calibri) and large (30+) fonts.
  • Have NO embedded videos.
  • Please use the following naming convention: PaperID.pdf, PaperID.pptx, where PaperID is the code provided by the system during your paper submission.

PUBLICATION OPPORTUNITIES

All accepted research papers will be subjected to rigorous review process and will be published electronically in the conference proceedings.

The best-selectedpapers will be considered for publication in:

  • Archives of Materials Science and Engineering, [ISSN: 1897-2764 (Print); 2300-8679 (Online)] (SCOPUS Index), https://archivesmse.org/resources/html/cms/MAINPAGE

ABOUT CASABLANCA CITY

Casablanca is the biggest city in Morocco located on the Atlantic Ocean. It is the capital of the region of Greater Casablanca and is the principal economic capital, the primary naval base. Anyone feel immediately at home here and will have an almost instinctive understanding of the life here. There are plenty of hotels in and around Casablanca. Despite of the size of your budget, you can find a superb place to enjoy a meal in this vibrant city. Casablanca is a cultural melting pot, a progressive city that welcomes visitors with open arms.
